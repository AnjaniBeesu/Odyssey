# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Odyssey aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive open-source project. We want people with different backgrounds, experiences, and skill levels to feel comfortable learning, contributing, asking questions, and sharing feedback.

Participation in Odyssey's issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is subject to this Code of Conduct.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and courtesy.
- Discuss ideas, implementation choices, and evidence without attacking individuals.
- Welcome questions, constructive criticism, and differing perspectives.
- Give feedback that helps others understand and improve their work.
- Respect people's time, boundaries, and levels of experience.
- Protect private information and respect the privacy of other participants.
- Accept reasonable feedback and moderation decisions.

You do not need to be an experienced developer to participate. Questions and good-faith mistakes are part of collaborative development.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Threats, targeted abuse, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Sexual harassment, sexualized attention, or inappropriate sexual comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions or contribution workflows.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern or participating in an investigation.
- Other conduct that creates an unsafe or persistently hostile project environment.

Disagreement about code, design, or project direction is acceptable when expressed constructively.

## Reporting a Concern

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer privately through the contact method listed on the repository's GitHub profile.

Please include enough context to understand the concern, such as relevant links, dates, or a description of what happened. Do not publish private information or sensitive evidence in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Information may be shared when necessary to investigate or respond to a report.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for reviewing reports and determining an appropriate response.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

- A private warning or request to stop the behavior.
- Removal of inappropriate comments or other contributions.
- Restrictions on participation in project spaces.
- Closure of discussions or pull requests that have become abusive or disruptive.
- Temporary or permanent exclusion from project participation in serious or repeated cases.

Responses will consider the nature and severity of the behavior, relevant context, and any repeated violations.

Retaliation against people who raise concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces when someone is representing or participating in the Odyssey community.

## Acknowledgment

By participating in the Odyssey project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful environment for everyone.
